# Standard Showdown prize boosters (2016–2019)

Reviewed 2026-10-08. All ten existing English product definitions now reference
`<set>-showdown` in magic-search-engine. These contain ordinary set printings,
not the later planeswalker-stamped `promo:promopack` cards. No new catalog SKUs
or identifiers are needed. The 2024 revival awards individual promos and is not
part of this booster family.

## Season contents

Every season has two nonfoil rares/mythics. The remaining slots are:

| Season | Total | Other slots | Ordinary set pool |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| KLD | 3 | One foil; approximately 1/33 packs replace it with a Masterpiece | BFZ, SOI, EMN, KLD (explicit OGW exclusion) |
| AKH | 4 | Nonfoil AKH full-art basic; foil of any rarity | BFZ, OGW, SOI, EMN, KLD, AER, AKH |
| HOU | 5 | Nonfoil HOU full-art basic; foil rare/mythic; foil common/uncommon or HOU full-art basic | BFZ, OGW, SOI, EMN, KLD, AER, AKH, HOU |
| XLN | 4 | Foil Rebecca Guay basic (PSS2); foil of any rarity (source conflict below) | KLD, AER, AKH, HOU, XLN |
| RIX | 4 | Foil Rebecca Guay basic (PSS2); foil of any rarity | KLD, AER, AKH, HOU, XLN, RIX |
| DOM | 4 | Foil DOM basic; foil of any rarity | KLD, AER, AKH, HOU, XLN, RIX, DOM |
| M19 | 4 | Foil Alayna Danner basic (PSS3); foil of any rarity | KLD, AER, AKH, HOU, XLN, RIX, DOM, M19 |
| GRN | 4 | Foil Alayna Danner basic (PSS3); foil of any rarity | XLN, RIX, DOM, M19, GRN |
| RNA | 4 | Foil GRN Ravnica Weekend basic (PRWK, A01–A10); foil of any rarity | XLN, RIX, DOM, M19, GRN, RNA |
| WAR | 4 | Foil RNA Ravnica Weekend basic (PRW2, B01–B10); foil of any rarity | XLN, RIX, DOM, M19, GRN, RNA, WAR |

The ordinary pools use the sets legal during each season, restricted to base-set
printings and excluding double-faced/meld cards. This is a set-membership rule,
not a query against today's Standard legality. Masterpieces are included only
for KLD: all 45 Zendikar Expeditions plus the 30 Kaladesh Inventions available
before Aether Revolt. No Invocations, later Inventions, Planeswalker Deck-only
cards, Buy-a-Box exclusives or special promo treatments are added to the ordinary
pool. Split/aftermath cards remain eligible as single physical cards.

## Sources and decisions

- [WPN: Standard Showdown and Holiday Buy-a-Box](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/sign-standard-showdown-holiday-buy-box)
  specifies the KLD three-card pack, OGW/double-faced exclusions and approximately
  one Masterpiece in 33 boosters. This primary source takes precedence over the
  wiki's 1/35 figure. Exclude OGW from both ordinary slots, following the pack
  exclusion footnote (the wiki's summary table only mentions the nonfoil slot).
- [WPN: Amonkhet Video Guide](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/amonkhet-video-guide)
  specifies four cards, including a nonfoil full-art land and any-rarity foil.
- [Contemporary HOU announcement transcription](https://www.izzetmtgnews.com/archives/44982)
  records the July 14 Wizards announcement: five cards, with an additional
  guaranteed foil rare/mythic and the HOU full-art land option in the low-rarity
  foil slot. The linked original Wizards article currently returns 404; it is
  also linked from Librarities with an archive reference.
- [WPN: Ixalan program changes](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/xln-5-changes-know-you-schedule)
  specifies two rares/mythics, a Rebecca Guay foil land, and one any-rarity foil.
  [WPN's kit preview](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/preview-your-xln-kits)
  instead calls that foil common/uncommon. We use the explicit program
  specification, corroborated by Librarities' four-card transcription. This
  disagreement remains a research caveat; the wiki table's five-card XLN row
  conflicts with both four-card descriptions and is not used.
- [WPN: Ixalan rotation](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/xln-5-things-you-need-know)
  confirms BFZ, OGW, SOI and EMN rotated out together.
- [Magic Librarities: Standard Showdown](https://www.magiclibrarities.net/1103-rarities-standard-showdown-promos-english-cards-index.html)
  transcribes XLN, RIX, M19, GRN, RNA and WAR slot contents and identifies the
  seasonal land printings. RNA and WAR use the **previous** Ravnica Weekend's
  guild lands, not their own set's Weekend lands.
- [MTG Wiki: Standard Showdown](https://mtg.wiki/wiki/Standard_Showdown)
  supplies the DOM ordinary foil-basic distinction and the program's DFC and
  post-KLD Masterpiece exclusions. Use it with the corrections above, rather
  than copying its table wholesale.
- [WPN: M19 promos](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/see-your-m19-promo-cards)
  confirms introduction of the Alayna Danner land cycle.

## Estimated collation and remaining research

Published slot categories are not published print-sheet frequencies. The models
reuse the generic 2:1 per-card rare/mythic weights and 12:5:3 foil rarity-group
weights; HOU's low foil uses 12:5. Lands and Masterpieces are uniform within their
specified pools. Those weights are estimates, not measured Showdown pull rates.
The any-rarity foil pool includes ordinary basics under the source's broad
wording; HOU's low foil instead uses its explicit full-art HOU basic restriction.
The base-set pool does not remove individually banned cards without evidence
that the printed packs were changed. Exact sheet weighting, duplicate behavior,
and possible curated omissions remain unverified. No unsupported cross-slot
color, set, or rarity correlation is imposed.

The substantive unresolved source conflict is the XLN foil-slot rarity wording.
The committed model follows the program announcement; a packaging/opening study
could resolve whether the kit preview describes a real restriction.
