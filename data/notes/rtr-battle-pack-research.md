# Return to Ravnica Booster Battle Pack

## Contents and model

The product contains two 22-card guild packets and two ordinary 15-card RTR boosters (74 gameplay cards). Its display contains twelve products. Each documented packet has eleven distinct spells, its Guildgate, five basics of each guild color, and no rare. Fixed packets live in magic-preconstructed-decks; sealed selection operates on whole packets.

The variable selection follows the existing Gatecrash Battle Pack convention: select two distinct guilds from the five documented lists, with equal weights. This is a modeling estimate, not measured factory collation. The evidence establishes multiple different-guild pairings but does not prove that all ten unordered pairs shipped or that their frequencies were equal. Exact basic-land artwork counts also remain unverified; the lists use the same RTR:* convention as Gatecrash. Neither convention establishes physical art allocation or production odds.

## Rakdos packet

One each: Grim Roustabout, Splatter Thug, Traitorous Instinct, Explosive Impact, Thrill-Kill Assassin, Deviant Glee, Bellows Lizard, Auger Spree, Rakdos Ringleader, Rakdos Shred-Freak, Catacomb Slug, Rakdos Guildgate; five Swamps and five Mountains.

Thrill-Kill Assassin and Rakdos Ringleader are the two uncommons. The tentative recollection of Dead Reveler in the forum is not used: it does not occur in this observed packet. Likewise, the remembered Dreg Mangler does not replace the observed Golgari uncommons. No extra packet variant is inferred from those recollections.

## Remaining evidence limits

Complete observed lists exist for all five guilds. Additional internal variants, factory pairing frequencies and exact basic-land artwork multiplicities have not been established. These limitations are documented alongside the implementation; the model does not claim reconstructed factory collation.
