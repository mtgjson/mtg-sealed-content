# Reality Fracture Bundle promos and remaining land lists

[Wizards' collecting guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-reality-fracture)
confirms one traditional-foil promo in the regular Bundle and two of ten
exclusive traditional-foil promos in the Secret Lair Bundle. Map the regular
Bundle directly to Carnivorous Cultivator // Enroot 437; its identity is
corroborated by [Magic Madhouse's Bundle-foil listing](https://magicmadhouse.co.uk/magic-the-gathering-reality-fracture-carnivorous-cultivator-enroot-bundle-foil).
The Secret Lair promo pack uses FRA 439–448: Enlightened Confidant, Return to
the Light Realms, Seasoned Cryomancer, Sphinx's Approach, The Theorist, Jace
Beleren, Darklight Phoenix, Overwrite the Multiverse, Craterclaw Colossus,
Stingcaster Mage, and Omnipresence. Individual weights are estimated equally;
the published two-of-ten description does not establish factory frequencies
or pair correlations. The card index and published MTGJSON card records verify
printing identity and foil availability, not collation.

The two missing land-pack decklists remain unresolved. The regular Bundle has
30 lands, split 15 foil/15 nonfoil. [GameStop's product specification](https://www.gamestop.com/toys-games/trading-cards/products/magic-the-gathering-reality-fracture-bundle/20035130.html)
further splits each finish into ten ordinary and five borderless lands.
Wizards specifies twenty traditional-foil tower basics in the Secret Lair
Bundle, but there are fifteen tower artworks in the set. Neither source gives
an exact allocation of those artworks within either product. Do not turn
these totals into invented fixed decklists. Existing unresolved references
remain visible until a complete land-pack inventory is available.
