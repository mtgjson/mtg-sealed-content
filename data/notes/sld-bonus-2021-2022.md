# Secret Lair bonus audit: 2021–2022

Random bonus pools belong in `taw/magic-search-engine/data/boosters/sld-bonus-*.yaml`.
Each referenced booster models the complete one-card bonus slot, including rare
replacements. This repository only selects the booster for each sealed drop.
Japanese variants retain their existing `copy` references. Main decks and card
counts are unchanged.

The companion search-engine PR adds 40 drop-specific boosters serving 76 variants.
Its `data/boosters/notes/sld-bonus-2021-2022.md` contains the membership table,
opening sources, and probability assumptions. Equal weights within categories
and the 5% Blueprint / 2% special-replacement conventions are estimates, not
published drop-specific odds. Strixhaven foil/nonfoil variants have separate
regular pools. The legacy shared land/Sliver boosters remain explicitly labelled
fallbacks for unresolved consumers; they do not establish per-drop eligibility.

The second evidence pass adds Just Add Milk, Look at the Kitties, Li'l'er Walkers,
and Pride Across the Multiverse, plus Hedron Archive in Jaya Ballard. Reports of
Root Sliver in Time Trouble Two and Scuttling Sliver in Kozyndan: Another Story
are useful leads but do not establish complete regular pools.

The full audit scope was 225 dated SLD variants plus Heads I Win, Tails You Lose.
Eight Secretversary 2020 variants with 2021 shipping dates were handled in the
previous batch. Replacement Heliod shipments are excluded.

Remaining evidence is tracked by drop year:
- [2021 follow-up](https://github.com/mtgjson/mtg-sealed-content/issues/743)
- [2022 follow-up](https://github.com/mtgjson/mtg-sealed-content/issues/744)

Merge and publish the companion booster definitions before rebuilding sealed
contents, so every newly referenced pack resolves downstream.
